## Techniques to Study

| Technique | Best Example | How to Use |
|-----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Reframing familiar idea** | "интернет это контент → интернет это рынок внимания" | Start with what people think, reveal the reality |
| **Structured list (numbered)** | "5 результатов обучения" | Use when explaining 3-5 concepts deeply |
| **Personal story with lesson** | Список про друга в Германии, ученик со StackOverFlow | End stories with explicit lesson |
| **Specific numbers** | "160 контактов, 11 купили, нужно 25" | Always use numbers instead of vague descriptors |
| **Systems analysis** | RTB bid request breakdown, market cycles | Show how the system works, not just what happens |
| **Short form wisdom** | "долгие отношения строятся не на яркости" | Use sparingly, for counter-intuitive insight |
| **Open questions** | "Расскажите, как у вас дела с AI?" | Invite reader thinking, not just agreement |
| **Code in context** | Redux serialization example | Explain why before showing code, integrate with narrative |
| **Honest admission** | "Это была первая ошибка" | Lead with the mistake, not the solution |

## What's Consistent Across All Examples

1. **Specific over vague** — always "160" not "many", "3 месяца" not "a while"
2. **Personal grounding** — even analysis starts with "я наткнулся", "недавно делал"
3. **Systems > stories** — tell the story to reveal the system, not vice versa
4. **Honest > polished** — show thinking, not just conclusions
5. **Invite > convince** — end with questions or frameworks, not arguments
6. **No filler** — every sentence earns its place
7. **Anti-hype voice** — skeptical of trends, curious about patterns

## Using These Examples

**When drafting:**
- Identify which type fits your idea
- Look at that example's structure
- Notice the tone and pacing
- Check how it opens, develops, and ends

**When revising:**
- Compare rhythm to similar examples
- Check for robotic transitions (your examples don't have them)
- Verify you have specific data/examples
- Make sure there's a system or insight, not just a story

**For reference:**
- When in doubt about tone, read the micro-wisdom examples
- When structuring complex ideas, reference the learning outcomes post
- When explaining technical concepts, check the Redux example
- When being honest about failure, see the Bootcamp post
