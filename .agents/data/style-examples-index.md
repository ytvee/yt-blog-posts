## What To Study

- how openings move from concrete situations to broader meaning
- how numbers are used to support authority
- how systems are explained without losing the personal voice
- how short-form posts compress judgment without sounding empty
- how failure posts stay honest without becoming self-pitying

## Usage Rule

These files are reference material. They are not templates to copy mechanically. Use them to understand rhythm, structure, and voice, then write an original post that fits the current topic.
