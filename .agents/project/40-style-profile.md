# Style Profile

Extracted from the saved post examples and the brand profile. Last updated: 2026-04-27.

## Core Tone

- calm
- personal
- expert without arrogance
- practical
- reflective
- anti-hype

## Voice Signals

- Start from lived experience, then expand into systems thinking.
- Prefer observation over performance.
- Use first-person narration naturally.
- Admit uncertainty, trade-offs, and failure when they matter.
- Respect the reader's intelligence and time.

## Pacing

- Paragraphs are usually short or medium.
- The reading rhythm is deliberate, not rushed.
- White space is used for breathing room and emphasis.
- Short sentences are used to sharpen a point, not to manufacture drama.

## Common Openings

- a personal observation
- a concrete situation from work
- a direct reframe of a familiar idea
- a simple question that opens a larger system

Examples:

- "Мы привыкли думать, что интернет — это контент. На самом деле интернет — это рынок."
- "Недавно делал на одном из проектов валидацию уникальных просмотров страниц пользователями."
- "Летом 2023 мы планировали запустить очный IT-буткемп городского формата."

## Common Structures

- observation -> reframe -> analysis -> practical takeaway
- experience -> mistake -> numbers -> lesson
- curiosity -> exploration -> explanation -> invitation to respond
- framework -> numbered breakdown -> synthesis

## Preferred Devices

- reframing a familiar idea
- specific numbers instead of vague claims
- personal reflection with concrete examples
- system analysis that reveals hidden incentives or mechanics
- honest postmortem writing
- questions that invite real discussion

## Avoid

- robotic transitions
- inflated summaries
- clickbait language
- generic motivation
- false urgency
- corporate polish

## Language Expectations

- write all operational guidance in English
- write posts in Russian unless the user asks otherwise
- preserve quoted Russian titles or excerpts only as examples
- keep the prose direct, readable, and specific

## Style Anchors

The saved references show five recurring modes:

- long-form educational authority posts
- reflective research posts
- technical problem-solution posts
- short-form market and reliability observations
- honest postmortem narratives
